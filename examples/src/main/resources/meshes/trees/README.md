| Files | Source |
|---|---|
| `fir-*.obj`, `leafy-*.obj`, `round-*.obj`, `autumn-*.obj` and `textures/trees/*.png` | [Low Poly Forest Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-forest-pack) by Ajay Karat, [Devil's Work.shop](http://devilswork.shop/) (`treeTall01-04`, `tree01-03`, `treeRoundTop01-03`, `treeBlob01-03`; the palette textures are downscaled to 256x256) |
| `pine.obj`, `pinetree.mtl` | [Low Poly Pine tree](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-pine-tree-0) by Aredon (the diffuse colors are darkened by 0.8 like the other trees) |

The pine has a material per part in its MTL file, so its object has no material of its own and is split into parts.
The other trees take their colors from a palette texture of their material.
